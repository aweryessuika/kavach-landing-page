Kavach — the EMI locker app for Indian mobile shops. Every phone you sell comes back paid.

Kavach (Kavach Lock) is an EMI locker app that protects every Android smartphone you sell on credit or EMI, and lets you lock or unlock it with one tap. It works on 120+ phone models including Samsung, Xiaomi, Redmi, Vivo, Oppo, Realme, Motorola and iQOO.

How Kavach works

01. Scan the box

Scan the IMEI barcode on the phone box and type the customer's name and number in your Kavach app.

02. Scan one QR on the new phone

Tap the welcome screen six times and scan the QR code. Protection is on in about 48 seconds, with no computer or cable.

03. Lock only when you decide

Nothing locks automatically. When a customer stops paying, tap lock in the Kavach app. When they pay, tap unlock. Factory reset, recovery mode, USB flashing and SIM swaps cannot remove Kavach, and a phone without internet can be unlocked with an offline PIN.

EMI locker price for your shop

You pay once per phone for the whole loan, with no monthly fee and no commission on your EMI. The per phone price depends on how many phones you sell and which brands, so Kavach quotes it on WhatsApp. The first 10 phones are free.
